Returns and refunds

Policy overview

Every velselle order comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If the Christmas Wonderland Projector is not right for you, send it back within 30 days of delivery and we refund the full price. If it arrives damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, we replace it or refund you, at no cost to you.

Return and exchange eligibility

Anyone who bought from us can return their order within 30 days of delivery, for any reason. Trying the projector at home is fine; that is what the 30 days are for. Please send it back complete, with the remote and the USB cable, in its box and without damage that happened after delivery. If you bought a 3-pack or a 5-pack and return only some of the projectors, we refund the price you paid per projector: $23.33 each from a 3-pack, $21.00 each from a 5-pack. Return all of them and you get the full order total back. There is no restocking fee.

Return window

You have 30 days from the day your order is delivered. The same 30 days apply to damaged, faulty or wrong items.

Return shipping responsibility

If you change your mind, you pay for the return shipping. Please use a tracked service so your package can be followed. If your item arrived damaged, faulty or wrong, we cover the return cost. If you paid a shipping fee, we refund it too, so nothing is deducted from your refund.

How to start a return or exchange

  1. Email support@velselle.com with your order number and a short note on why you are returning it.
  2. We reply within one business day with the return address and instructions.
  3. Pack the projector with its remote and cable, send it with tracking, and reply with your tracking number.

Please write to us before sending anything back, so we can match your package to your order.

Damaged or defective items

If your projector arrives damaged, stops working or is not what you ordered, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo or short video of the problem. We send a replacement or refund you, your choice. If we need the item back, we cover the cost.

Cancellations

You can cancel any time before your order ships for a full refund. Email us as soon as possible with your order number. Once your order has shipped, our 30-day guarantee still applies.

Refund processing time

As soon as your return is back with us, we refund the full price, or the per-projector amount above, to your original payment method, and we email you when it is done. Your bank or card usually shows the refund a few business days later.

Our mistake vs. your change of mind

If the mistake is ours (a damaged, faulty or wrong item), you pay nothing: we send a replacement or a refund and cover any return shipping. If you simply change your mind, you get the full price back and pay only the return shipping.

Exchanges

The projector comes in one model and one color, so an exchange means a replacement for a damaged, faulty or wrong item, shipped to you free. If you would like more projectors, simply place a new order.

We are here to help

We want your projector to make this Christmas brighter. If anything is not right, write to support@velselle.com. A real person reads every email and replies within one business day.
