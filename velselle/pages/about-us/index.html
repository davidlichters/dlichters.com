About velselle

velselle brings Christmas light into your home without the ladder. Our Christmas Wonderland Projector turns a wall or a ceiling into a slow, moving scene of Santa's sleigh, reindeer, snowmen and falling snowflakes, from a small white projector that sits on a nightstand. We make it for families who want the whole house to feel like Christmas, and for anyone looking for a gift that suits every age on their list.

What sets us apart is focus. Instead of a catalog of hundreds of decorations, we put all of our attention on a single product. That lets us tell you exactly what you are buying, ship it on a schedule you can plan around, and answer your questions in plain words.

Our mission and values

Christmas lives in small moments: the kids' room at bedtime, the living room on movie night, the hallway when guests arrive. We want every one of those rooms to glow, without extension cords across the floor or strings of lights to untangle every December. Three values guide how we work. We describe our product exactly as it is. We deliver when we say we will. And when something goes wrong, we put it right quickly and without fuss.

What we specialize in

The Christmas Wonderland Projector casts 16 colorful holiday patterns, including Santa's sleigh, reindeer, snowmen, snowflakes, Christmas trees, candy canes and gifts. The patterns turn slowly across the room, and a small remote lets you change the speed, the light mode and the brightness, or set a timer so the projector switches itself off after bedtime. It runs on USB from a phone charger, a power bank or a laptop, and the ball tilts on its round base, so you can aim it at a wall or straight up at the ceiling.

We are just as clear about what is not in the box. The USB wall adapter and the two AAA batteries for the remote are not included, and we tell you before you order, not after. The size, the contents of the box and answers to the most common questions are all on the product page.

If it arrives damaged or faulty, send us a photo within the same 30 days and we send a replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer.
