Will it arrive before Christmas?

Order today, arrives in 13 to 25 days. We pack your order within 2 to 5 business days, then it arrives within 7 to 12 business days. Order by for Christmas delivery. Every order ships with tracking.

How big is the projection?

The scene grows with the distance to the wall or ceiling, and the projector works from about 6.5 to 20 ft (2 to 6 m) away. It is brightest in a dark room. The photos on this page show styled scenes, so size and brightness in your room depend on distance and darkness.

Does it need a wall outlet?

It runs on USB, so a USB phone charger, a power bank or a laptop works. The Type-C cable is included; a USB wall adapter is not.

What does the remote do?

It changes the rotation speed, the light mode and the brightness, and it sets the auto-off timer. It takes 2 AAA batteries, which are not included.

How do the 3-pack and 5-pack work?

Choose 3 or 5 above and the discount is applied automatically in your cart, no code needed: 3 projectors cost $69.99 ($23.33 each), 5 cost $104.99 ($21.00 each).

What is in the box?

The projector, the remote control and a Type-C USB cable (about 39 in, 1 m).

Where do you ship?

To the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, always with tracking. Orders of 2 or more projectors ship free.

What if it isn't right for me?

If the projector is not right for you, send it back within 30 days of delivery and we refund the full price. Arrived damaged or not working? Send us a photo within the same 30 days and we send a replacement or refund you, your choice.

Is it a good gift?

Yes. It needs no setup and runs from an ordinary USB charger, so it suits grandparents and kids alike. Order by for Christmas delivery.
