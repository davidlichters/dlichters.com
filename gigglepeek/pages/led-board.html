Questions before you order

How big is the board?

About 12 x 8 in (30 x 20 cm). It stands upright on its stand on a desk, shelf or nightstand.

How is it powered?

With the USB cable: plug it into a USB wall charger, a laptop or a power bank. There is no battery to charge.

How do I clean it?

Wipe it with water or a damp cloth. For bright lines that hold on, wipe once more with a little water.

Can they trace pictures?

Yes. Lay a coloring page behind the clear board and trace the lines with the neon markers.
