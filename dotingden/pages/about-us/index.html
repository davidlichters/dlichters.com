About DotingDen

DotingDen makes nail day easier for cats, dogs and the people who love them. Our first tool is a 2-in-1 nail clipper and grinder that lights up the nail with two LEDs, catches the clippings in a clear cap and smooths the edges with a quiet grinder built into the other end. It is made for adult cats and small to medium dogs.

Why one tool

Most people trim at home with a plain clipper, then pick nail pieces off the floor, and then reach for a second gadget to file the sharp tips. Our clipper does both steps in one hand: clip a little at a time, turn it around and smooth. The clear catcher keeps the clippings in one place, and the grinder hums at about 55 dB, softer than a normal conversation.

What we tell you straight

On light-colored nails the LEDs show the pink quick, so you can see where to stop. On dark nails the light shows the surface only, so take tiny cuts and finish with the grinder. We would rather say that up front than have you find out halfway through a trim.
