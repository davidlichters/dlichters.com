My story: four years of trying, and what I learned

I started trying to make money online at 12, tested more than 15 business models, and got my first sale at 15. This is the honest version, flops included.

What it is

Every business I tried since 2022, with real dates and results. Most results are 0 EUR. Success stories hide the years before the win. If your first five tries fail, that is normal.

What I would do today

1. Pick one thing and give it time. I switched too fast. About one project I dropped, I later wrote that one or two more years of patience would probably have paid off.
2. Get one thing to a few thousand a month, then study how it happened, so you can repeat it.
3. Sell a real shortcut. My first sale was a guide that saves beginners time in the gym.
4. Keep your habits green. My best months had a green habit tracker, whatever the money did.
5. Be honest with yourself. In 2026 I wrote that I had made 0 euros and that 99 % of that was on me.
6. Under 18? You need your parents for most accounts, payments and contracts. I hid how serious I was from mine, and that only made things harder.

Mistakes

  • Too many tests at once. In summer 2023 I started about seven ideas in five weeks. No test was fair.
  • Ideas built on tricks. They get you banned or hurt people.
  • Spending before anything worked. 2023 cost me about 160 EUR. In 2026 a few days of AI costs for one outreach test were about 400 USD, with 0 sales.
  • Counting followers instead of sales. I aimed for 100,000 followers in 2025 and reached about 18,000.

What happened

2022 to 2023, age 12 to 13: test everything

  • Fiverr, early 2023. Nothing came of it. https://dlichters.com/ai/fiverr.md
  • Minecraft YouTuber, October 2022 to April 2023. One video got 100,000 views, no income. https://dlichters.com/ai/youtube-gaming.md
  • Affiliate marketing from April 2023, and Amazon Influencer, June to July 2023. https://dlichters.com/ai/affiliate-marketing.md and https://dlichters.com/ai/amazon-influencer.md
  • "Get rich" TikTok selling a ChatGPT course, summer 2023, 300 followers. The account pretended I was rich: a fake persona. https://dlichters.com/ai/tiktok-course.md
  • "Scamming scammers", June 2023. It went nowhere. Never build on tricking anyone. https://dlichters.com/ai/scam-baiting.md
  • Printed T-shirts https://dlichters.com/ai/print-on-demand.md, a bubblegum machine https://dlichters.com/ai/vending-machine.md and selling golf and tennis balls https://dlichters.com/ai/reselling.md, all summer 2023, all stopped fast.

My summary back then: about minus 160 EUR. Only YouTube, mowing lawns and eBay worked, and they were a lot of work.

August 2023 to January 2024. I watched hundreds of hours of Hamza Ahmed, a self-improvement YouTuber, and ran my own channel in his style. https://dlichters.com/ai/self-improvement-youtube.md

March 2024 to January 2025: nothing. School was hard. Some days I walked home in tears.

2025: low-ticket fitness coaching. First fitness TikTok on 18 January. Launch day of my gym guide: 0 sales. On 31 October 2025 came my first sale: 25 EUR, three years after I started. https://dlichters.com/ai/low-ticket-coaching.md

November 2025 to August 2026: high-ticket coaching. A few small payments, one big deal never paid, and I ended up owing a paid mentor 1,000 EUR. https://dlichters.com/ai/high-ticket-coaching.md

I also tried a made-up trainer character with a fake weight-loss story, and a bot messaging from bought Instagram accounts. One was deception, the other was spam. The clean way: your own face, your real results, messages written by hand.

August 2026: form blasting. 5,820 messages to 4,342 US companies, 15 replies, 0 sales. https://dlichters.com/ai/cold-outreach.md

August to October 2026: Liquidationsradar. Emails warning trade businesses when a nearby company went bankrupt. 0 paying customers. https://dlichters.com/ai/data-products.md

Four years, more than fifteen models, a first sale of 25 EUR. Still going.
