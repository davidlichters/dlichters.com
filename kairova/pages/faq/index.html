Questions and answers

What age is it for?

Ages 8 and up. The straps adjust, so parents and grandparents can play too.

How does it work?

Each player straps on a chest target and picks a team colour. Hit a player from another team and their target lights up, sounds and counts down a life. Only opposing colours can score.

Does it need batteries?

Yes, 3 AAA batteries per blaster and per chest target, not included: 12 for 2 players, 24 for 4 players.

Can we play outside?

Yes, in the garden, the park or anywhere with space to run. The range is shorter in bright sunlight.

When will my order arrive?

Order today, arrives in 14 to 26 days. We pack your order within 2 to 3 business days, then it arrives within 8 to 15 business days. Every order ships with tracking.

Is it safe?

It uses infrared light only, no real lasers. For ages 8 and up.

Can we add more players later?

Yes. Every set plays with every other set, up to four team colours.

What if it isn't right for us?

If the set isn't right for your family, send it back within 30 days of delivery and we refund the full price.

Is it a good gift?

Yes. It is a gift the whole family plays together. Order by for Christmas delivery.
