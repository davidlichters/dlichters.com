### Chargebacks, Website-Konvertierung und TikTok-Verkaufsstrategien (Course C) [L0352, 2022]

- [15:54-16:48] Multi-language store: the speakers observed that Spanish-speaking countries tend to be lower income and "don't tend to buy as much" as English-speaking countries; for Spanish speakers inside the US, build the store in English first and add Shopify's translate option. (2022)
- [37:24-39:14] In Spain (student's report) shoppers are less used to "Shop now", PayPal or Google Pay buttons; stores there commonly use a cash-on-delivery order button (pay on arrival, fold-out form for details). Speaker: button wording is a preference; test it. Their own US setup: "Add to cart" on top, "Shop now/Buy now" below it. (2022)
- [24:43-25:36] PayPal case: a student had 600 pounds withheld by PayPal. [30:38-31:30] A friend scaling in Q4 had PayPal suddenly hold his funds, leaving him without money to pay his supplier for inventory in the middle of Q4. Speaker deactivates PayPal for this reason. (2022)
- [56:55-58:45] Spark ads split-test result: two accounts (one only for Spark ads, one only organic, on separate phones): the organic account performed better on views, the Spark ads account did better on sales and conversion "because you can target". After running Spark ads, the speaker noticed later videos on that account did not perform as well (cause not certain). (2022)
- [44:46-45:35] For the Halloween slides product, the speaker advises launching next year in September, "early September", and notes that late entrants run into shipping times and buyers who already saw many copycats. (2022)

### Dezember 2019 Coaching Call - Q&A zu Zahlungen, Team und Skalierung (Course A) [L0467, 2019]

- [19:24 to 20:01] Holiday site elements: a coupon code unique to Christmas; a site-wide red stripe/banner ("Christmas sale ... enter coupon code CHRISTMAS to check out") seen by first-time Google Ads visitors; a homepage image slider if used.
- [1:11:39] Supplier without MAP policy: search the supplier's products in Google Shopping; if sellers visibly make only about 10% margin, do not sell them; if sellers are extremely profitable, sell them (and exclude them from your price-match policy).
- [27:39 to 28:31] Owners of brick-and-mortar retail: no complications joining the model; one Hawaii-retreat member's physical store gave connections to brands that refuse online-only retailers, so he could sell for brands most online-only stores cannot; when calling suppliers, say you call from the named store at its location to secure even more suppliers; if the store's niche is outside the sweet spot, find related products that fit better.
