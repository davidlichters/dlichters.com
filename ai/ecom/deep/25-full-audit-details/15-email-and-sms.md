### Black Friday Funnel mit E-Mail List Building (Course C) [L0307, 2023+]

- [09:20] The lead campaign runs continuously from November 1 to the end of November, collecting leads even during Black Friday. (the welcome series fires Day 1/2/3 after each sign-up.)
- [21:13] Comparison: list building still gets leads at 2.95 while a purchase campaign in the same season will not get a purchase for less than $20.
- [19:33] Case result: a mainly physical-retail brand spent almost 4k on the Black Friday list-building strategy, with a profit margin of about 65%; the same lead-and-blast strategy is reused for Christmas, January sales, winter sales and summer sales.
- [20:23] Optional incentives on top: bundles, upsells, one-time offers, free shipping on orders above $50 plus a free gift. If doing this for the first time, keep it simple and skip them.
- [07:59] Jewelry brand version of the offer: "Black Friday private sale, up to 50% discount", landing page with a button that opens a pop-up form.
- [25:14] Purchased list example: about 50,000 emails; upload the CSV to Facebook or Google, target that audience AND build lookalikes from it, send both to the opt-in page.
- [28:01] Sending to a cold or bought list from the main domain: about 60% chance it ends up unopened and damages the domain, so never use the main domain name for it.
- [27:12] Supplement client started with $500 for the whole project and ran at about 1k per month, almost break-even, before slowly building up to the later numbers.
- [52:00] Teacher's rule: max $300 per product; if after $300 the product is not profitable or only at break-even, cut it.
- [52:55] Second mentor (Mentor C4): more patience, first find a creative with high CTR; a meme-style picture with the product beat a branded video; kill the product after $300 only if CTR is low.
- [23:40] Student case: about 9,000 emails held in an Excel sheet; advice was to re-engage via ad upload and re-opt-in page, not to email them from the main domain.
- [31:43] The transcript only says "a bundle worth $500 with a coaching program and one-to-one sessions done by VAs" (no 8 weeks).
- [38:07] Teacher planned a benefit-led tea brand (focus, energy, libido tea, bundles and subscriptions, white-label factory in India) for Q3 2023.
