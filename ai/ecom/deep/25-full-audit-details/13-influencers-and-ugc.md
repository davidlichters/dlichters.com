### Alternative Traffic: Micro-Influencer, Content Marketing und die Reise zu organischem Wachstum (Course A) [L0226, 2017]

- [17:54-19:27] Get content ideas from top players' blogs in the niche (REI for kayaks/paddleboards, e.g. "build your own kayak cart"; Home Depot-style home blogs; Wayfair user guides): copy the idea, not the content. (2017)
- [20:13-20:59] Write the Black Friday / Cyber Monday deals article already in August so it ranks by November; the article need not be linked in the blog navigation, it only has to be live and indexable. (2017)
- [21:46] Values/cause articles: the teacher donated business profit after a disaster in Texas and wrote an article about it, to share values and build awareness, not to sell. (2017)
- [23:19] Trade-show content: if you cannot attend, ask a supplier exhibiting there to photograph your logo with the products at the show and publish an article about it. (2017)
- [24:05] Install product-page Q&A apps (customers ask, others or you answer, Amazon-style): free content that helps SEO and informs buyers; free versions exist in the Shopify App Store. (2017)
- [24:52] Expert-only tactic: build a niche authority site (external blog about the niche); once it has traffic, link to your store carefully to avoid penalties and earn from competitors via ads and affiliate programs; takes a lot of time and effort. (2017)
- [28:27-29:13] Master both Google and Bing webmaster tools, not just for sitemaps: check errors and fix them as soon as possible; submit every new product page, article or blog post via Crawl > "Fetch as Google" (old Search Console UI), otherwise indexing can take weeks or months. (2017, possibly outdated interface)
- [28:27] Write appealing title and meta description for Google results because more clicks lead to higher rankings later. (2017)
- [30:36] YouTube: ask suppliers for product videos, add your logo and free music (or even slideshows) and publish with good titles/descriptions; for more professional videos outsource on Fiverr. (2017)
- [31:27] Pinterest pins added to your boards let you replace the pin's link with your own URL (backlink); teacher unsure whether this is allowed. (2017)
- [15:11] One year after starting content, organic became the #1 traffic source and referral fell to #2/#3 (2016 start was mostly referral). (2017)
- [34:28] Collect emails via giveaways, coupons and free accessories; the teacher sends multiple emails per week on top of email funnels (new blog posts, new products, holiday promos, industry news). (2017)
