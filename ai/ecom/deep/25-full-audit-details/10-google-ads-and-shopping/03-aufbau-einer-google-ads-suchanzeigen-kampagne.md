### Aufbau einer Google Ads Suchanzeigen-Kampagne (Course B) [L0262, 2021-22]

- [03:23] If your search-text budget is restricted to single-digit dollars per day (e.g. $2 or $5), do not run search text ads; put that money back into Shopping campaigns and grow there first.
- [04:06] With manual CPC, if conversions are occurring at some level you can switch on Enhanced CPC for "a little performance edge", or leave it off.
- [13:32] Keyword source for product-ID search campaigns: search terms pulled from the low-priority / high-value search-term report of the Shopping campaign.
- [28:40] After launch, Google can take a few hours to find where the auction lies; then it may warn that bids are below the first-page bid.
- [26:38] Even non-buying clickers enter the retargeting cookie pool and may opt in to the email list.

### Break-even ROAS und Google-Ads-Metriken berechnen (Course A) [L0327, 2020-26]

- [03:52] Conversion rate in the CPA example: 500 clicks, 18 conversions, $500 spend = $27.78 CPA and a 3.6% conversion rate.
- [06:31] A 10x ROAS ($100 spend for $1,000 revenue) says nothing about profitability until COGS is known ("Are we profitable or not? Who knows?").
- [12:39] Target ROAS rule of thumb used in the course: decide what % of profit-before-ads to spend (50% in the example), not a % of revenue ("not $1,000 times 50%").

### Deine maximale Ausgabe pro Verkauf (Course B) [L0446, 2021-22]

- [07:19] Ideal target cost per sale is framed as "instead of breaking even, 20% profit in your pocket".

### Der Aufstieg der PPC-Automatisierung (Course A) [L0454, 2019]

- [22:50] How to raise the target ROAS: if the account is at 3x ROAS, set the portfolio target to 5x first, and only then to 8x, not straight to 8x or 10x. Bigger jumps cause more volatility at the start.
- [28:09] Teacher's estimate: a couple of years before 2019 automated bidding beat manual setups about 10 to 20% of the time, and that share rises over time.
- [34:03] eBay "gets searched more than Google Shopping, believe it or not" (reason to push the feed to eBay).
- [40:56] Prediction: in three years Smart Shopping will always beat traditional Alpha Beta campaigns, but at the time it is volatile with lots of frustration at launch.
- [38:52] Public poll answers quoted (May 2019): one manager sees Smart Shopping beat standard campaigns about 80% of the time; others report it outperforming; one dislikes not being able to exclude placements.
- [19:05] Example of manual-only cost: optimizing the 2 billion bids by spreadsheet would take about 20 hours for one keyword.
- [32:37] Title test cadence: check the search term report and put converting terms in product titles about once a quarter (4 times a year).

### Die vier Steine der PPC-Skalierung (Course A) [L0510, 2018]

- [12:05]-[12:58] Mobile: about one in three e-commerce sales happen on mobile, mobile add-to-carts up 25% year over year; mobile getting ~80% of clicks is not the reason a shop is not converting (mobile converts well).
- [57:07]-[58:39] Mobile vs desktop: lower-priced products tend to convert faster on the same device; check desktop and mobile conversion rate "every 60 days" or with a monthly reminder; device bid adjustments must be set at campaign level, so segment products into separate campaigns. Cross-device tracking improving.
- [43:13]-[46:19] For products without search volume (e.g. novel gadget/sweatshirt) shopping ads cannot start the funnel; start with top-of-funnel Facebook, best with influencers talking about the product, which complicates the funnel (influencer payout, afterthought funnel).
- [46:19]-[47:50] Email deliverability answer: speaker is not an email expert; general tips are personalized, less promotional emails and a reason to open (e.g. coupon), opens signal Google/Gmail.
- [15:37] Google gets 95% of revenue and Facebook 90% from ads, so both reward clickable ads (background).
- [29:39] Facebook ads setup has about 12 options per screen and roughly 200 options for a simple ad vs Google Shopping simple create.
- [36:35] Goal is not the lowest CPC but to bid as high as possible than competitors (they cannot match $1.41 max bid vs their $1 cap).
- [54:47] Quality score exists at keyword, ad group, campaign and advertiser level; only keyword level shown (and none for shopping).
- [1:01:00] YouTube targeting of channels with ~200 subscribers.
- [41:42] Search Scientist offers PPC management mainly for budgets over $5,000 per month.
