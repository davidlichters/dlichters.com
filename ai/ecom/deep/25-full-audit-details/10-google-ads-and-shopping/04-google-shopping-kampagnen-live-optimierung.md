## 10. Google Ads and Shopping (page 4 of 4)

### Google Shopping Kampagnen Live-Optimierung (Course A) [L0745, 2020-26]

- [12:32] Pause rule example: at a 2.59% conversion rate expect a sale every 38 clicks; a product with about 70 clicks and no conversion (roughly twice 38) is "time to go".
- [14:05] "Having data" means about 15, at least 10 conversions a month before applying this rule.
- [29:05] Device bid adjustments: only adjust mobile when data is thin; tablets with 29 clicks are too few to judge (mobile example: 3.55 / 6 = 0.59, minus 1 = 41% decrease). Only the 41% was found, not the tablet/29 clicks reasoning.
- [30:47] Portfolio target ROAS (only for campaigns with over 30 conversions per month): at 30 conversions/month it takes about two months to optimize properly; with 60 or 120 conversions per month about one month. Min/max bid fields left empty.
- [23:57] Geo bid adjustment filter: only use regions with over 100 clicks; do not decide on 30 clicks unless conversion rate is about 3.3% or more.

### Google Shopping Kampagnen mit Alpha-Beta-Struktur aufbauen (Course A) [L0746, 2020-26]

- [21:14] Beta bid ladder if no traffic after a week: 20 cents start, then 25 cents, let it run a week, then 30 cents, and work up from there.
- [27:00] Do not edit per-product bids at the start; let the campaigns run and check back after a week to see whether bids are in the right spot.
- [18:10] Launch budget: same budget for alpha and beta, about $20 each per day to get data in (demo $15 each, 27-product merch store).
- [13:44] Country tip: US store that may sell to Canada later still targets only the United States; make separate alpha and beta campaigns per additional country, never multiple countries in the main campaign.

### Google Shopping-Kampagnen mit Custom Labels strukturieren (Course B) [L0747, 2021-22]

- [05:18] Segmentation has no direct bearing on campaign performance: Google does not care how many campaigns, ad groups or products per ad group; only an indirect effect through how easy/frustrating management is. (Not found anywhere.)
- [16:23] CSV trick: after pasting Vendor/Title into the Custom Label columns, the pasted column header must be changed back to the "Custom Label 0/1" field name, otherwise the upload fails.
- [10:43] Never change product identifiers once ads run (Google sees a new product and history is lost) unless performance is really terrible.

### Google Shopping optimieren: Produktauswahl vor Gebotserhöhung (Course A) [L0748, 2020-26]

- [14:02] Do not block search terms just because they have no clicks yet; block only terms you do not want traffic for.

### Google Shopping Skalieren mit Kennzahlen (Course A) [L0750, 2020-26]

- [03:46] Negative keywords: do them at least every two weeks (teacher does weekly) once ads get impressions and clicks; the "Keywords" screen shows only negatives, the actual search terms are under Insights and reports > Search terms, one campaign at a time (alpha and beta). Only the "weekly" rule was found, not the "at least every two weeks".
- [14:29] Third product example: 20% search impression share and no clicks = a product you want to sell, so raise its max CPC to get impressions.
- [17:20] 100K club: members with $100,000+ sales say it took about a month before momentum built, because they spent that month dialing in their ads.
- [05:11] New campaigns usually need two to three days before impressions and clicks start.

### Google Such-Text-Anzeigen: Struktur und Strategie (Course B) [L0751, 2021-22]

- [01:18] Anatomy: landing page URL / display URL, ad headline, limited ad copy, plus extensions (location, phone number, product details, reviews) that Google does not always show and whose format varies.
- [06:48] You tell Google the search terms and you write the entire ad copy yourself (unlike Shopping where Google builds it from the feed); three questions to settle first: right time to start, right terms, how to write the ad.
- [06:05] Search text ads have a higher CPC than shopping ads "at least 80% of the time", so they need a decent conversion rate; top-of-funnel terms may force running at a loss for some time.
