### Course A - Refund Page Prompt (Dokument) (Course A) [L0539, undated]

- Persona: professional eCommerce customer-experience copywriter; policy for high-ticket products from domestic suppliers; trust-building, avoid legal jargon, never mention "dropshipping".
- Length 400-650 words; tone: professional, reassuring, transparent, supportive, confident; short section headers, simple paragraphs, no fluff/hype/templates.
- 11 sections: Policy Overview; Return & Exchange Eligibility; Return Window; Return Shipping Responsibility; How to Start a Return or Exchange (RMA Process); Damaged or Defective Items; Cancellations; Refund Processing Time; Our Error vs. Customer-Initiated Returns; Exchanges Process; Final Reassurance Statement.
- 14 student inputs with example values: return window 30 days from delivery; condition new/unused/original packaging/all accessories; restocking fee none; buyer's-remorse return shipping paid by customer; defective/wrong item shipping paid by store; original shipping cost refunded; refund processing within 7 business days after item received and inspected; cancellation full refund if canceled before shipping; freight (LTL) damage reporting window 48 hours after delivery; small parcel (UPS/FedEx) damage reporting 15 days after delivery; exchange shipping: customer pays return and reshipment; prepaid return labels yes, cost deducted from refund.
- Output rules: no placeholders, no explanations/notes, only the finished policy, unique each generation.

### Course A - Shipping Page Prompt (Dokument) (Course A) [L0541, undated]

- Persona: professional eCommerce operations copywriter; high-ticket products from domestic suppliers; never mention "dropshipping"; not robotic/templated; written for customers not lawyers.
- Length 300-500 words; tone: clear, professional, reassuring, trust-building, straightforward; simple paragraphs with short headers.
- 8 sections: Shipping Coverage Area; Order Confirmation; Order Processing Time; Shipment Notification & Tracking; Estimated Delivery Time; Shipping Carriers; Delays & Exceptions; Contact Information.
- Student inputs with examples: shipping region "Continental United States only"; handling 3-5 business days; transit 3-7; total 7-12; partners FedEx, UPS, USPS, freight carriers; freight for large items yes; optional white-glove/curbside ("curbside available for oversized items"); out-of-stock/custom orders: customer contacted with updated timelines; delays: proactively notify customers.
- Output rules: no placeholders, no explanations, only finished policy, unique each time.
