### 5.1 Product Page Optimizations (Dokument) (Course A) [L0139, undated]

- The expiring coupon is a "day-one requirement for all stores". (document)
- The coupon shown on the page must match the active Shopify discount; the code works at checkout regardless of the countdown; test different percentages over time. (document)
- Estimated delivery dates update automatically every night at midnight based on the current day; no maintenance needed. (document)
- Estimated delivery reduces purchase hesitation and cuts inbound "when will it arrive?" questions. (document)
- Per-supplier template click path: theme editor > Default product > Create template > name after the supplier (example "Brand B") > based on Default product > Save; then edit Estimated delivery on that template (example 7-14 days) > Save; then Shopify Admin > Products > open a product of that supplier > Theme template > choose "Brand B" > Save. (document)
- Product templates can also vary the coupon display and other layout/content per supplier group, not only delivery dates. (document)

### Branded Product Pages mit PageFly und ChatGPT (Course C) [L0321, undated]

- [12:29-13:22] ChatGPT copy workflow: first give as much product info as possible (brand type, product, benefits taken from a competitor store), then assign the role "you are a copywriter", ask for a one-line USP that shows the benefit and why to buy, then ask to make it less than 10 words.
- [17:40] Benefit-bullet prompt: "provide me four bullet points that describe benefit of the product; remember I need benefit and not feature"; if two are good, ask "generate more like these" for four more.
- [27:16] Fill-in prompt for social proof line: "complete this line for our product: 1000+ happy customers using [product] to change ..." (results like "boost their confidence and transform their skin").
- [30:05] Paragraph under the pain-point headline: ask ChatGPT for a small paragraph of three lines only; bold key words such as the product name.
- [32:37-43:37] Replacement method for every template section: paste the other product's (Shaper Loop) paragraph/features/closing line and ask "generate a similar paragraph/points for [my product] with exact tone or feeling"; then ask for a one- to two-line explanation per feature point.
- [44:33] Brand comparison: ChatGPT gave five points, teacher asked for three and used those (plus free shipping, 30-day money-back guarantee, store name instead of template name, own email in guarantee section).
- [20:28-21:21] Bundle apps in PageFly: drag an HTML/Liquid element directly after the add-to-cart button and paste the bundle app's code (e.g. Bundle Bear); delete the template's two bundle HTML sections if not used.
- [40:50] ezgif resize: if the GIF is too big, click Resize and set e.g. 600x600 or 800x800 before optimizing.
- [22:57] Known PageFly issue: button hover/selected state color may not change; workaround is to import a new button element or ask PageFly chat support.
- [51:18-53:09] PageFly cannot copy a single section between pages; instead duplicate the whole page and assign the copy as home page (or another product/collection) in PageFly settings, then edit.
- [1:04:11] Template import: Import > upload file > tick "I understand" that images will not be imported (images usually import anyway); then duplicate and edit.
- [06:10] Template example product (Shaper Loop / weighted hula hoop) was 6-7 months on the market and still working on TikTok; give your product a unique name different from other dropshippers' names.
- [36:35] The 7% conversion page's product was banned on TikTok after about 3 days because ads showed excessive skin and weight-loss claims; weight loss is risky in ads.
- [1:09:21-1:11:15] Ad-side test rule: if CTR is poor after day 1 or 2, launch a new campaign/ad group with new creatives for one more day, then switch the product; set a fixed budget per product test (example: not more than $200).
- [1:13:04] People who scale to $1,000/day from day 2 accept losing that money; publicly shown $2,000-10,000/day winners often succeeded in only 2-3 of 100 tests and run ~100 ad accounts, credit lines and multiple payment gateways built over 4-5 years.
- [1:17:29] Facebook custom content should show faces (e.g. compilation of multiple face-to-camera reviews; someone showing the product and explaining how it works); teacher recommends YouTuber Dara Denney for Facebook creative structure.
- [1:15:43] Own content gets better CPMs; replicate viral videos that already work rather than innovating; teacher (in India) cannot get product within 2-3 days so uses existing recent content.
- [1:06:03] Unique new products often sell on first visit (50% or 80% off deal); for considered products add a pop-up and email setup to recover visitors.

### Course A - Demo Product Description Prompt (Dokument) (Course A) [L0535, undated]

- Persona "professional eCommerce copywriter for high-conversion Shopify product pages for premium products"; upload a demo product image, infer details and make realistic assumptions (product not real yet).
- Task 1, product name: premium-sounding, brandable/high-end, matches product type, no trademarked or existing brand names, professional and trustworthy.
- Task 2, description structure: short benefit-driven opening paragraph; "Key Features" bullet list of 5-8 bullets; "Why You'll Love It"/"Benefits" section; realistic "Specifications" section; short closing paragraph encouraging purchase.
- Tone: professional, confident, clear, premium, Shopify-ready, for conversion not fluff. Formatting: headings, bullets, easy to skim, no emojis, no excessive hype. Goal: impress suppliers, make product look store-ready.
