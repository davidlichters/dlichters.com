## 14. SEO and Content

Main chapter: https://dlichters.com/ai/ecom/deep/14-seo-and-content.md

### Bilder-Optimierung für SEO und visuelle Suche (Course B) [L0301, 2021-22]

- [14:58] Reseller alternatives to stock photos: get the product yourself and shoot with a smartphone, or travel to a dealer's showroom to take your own photos and video. Unique video is also valuable.
- [15:46] Retro-optimizing existing images: not urgent, do it bit by bit, start with the focus pages you most want to rank and work outward; this may require re-uploading the images.
- [10:38] Avoid image files of about 2 MB on a page; there is no magic file-size number, compress "to the point where quality becomes affected", balancing page speed against conversion (bad images lower conversion rate).

### Blog Posts SEO Optimierung (Course B) [L0311, 2021-22]

- [03:10] Once a site is a few years old and has a bank of content, expect to spend more time updating existing content than producing new content; continual yearly updating is what keeps ranking content at the top and makes it hard for a competitor to take the position.
- [00:00 / 14:50] Workflow rule: hand the blog-post checklist to whoever posts the content (writer, owner or a separate team member) and make running through it part of their workflow for every new post, and use it to work backwards through already-published posts.
- [11:22] Pop-ups/interstitials: teacher's stronger view is no pop-up on a blog post at any point ("or ever"), not only within the first 30 seconds.

### Content-Marketing Aktionsplan (Course B) [L0381, 2021-22]

- [03:18-04:39] Do not agonize over which of the 12 content ideas comes first ("Don't even have that conversation. Just get started... Pick a spot"). Example: with 12 product types on the store, nail ONE product type first (produce all content on that type), then move on to the next later; the order does not matter.
- [02:34] Fill in the attention matrix and do the keyword research in step one; it does not need to be finished this month, "make a substantial dent"; a VA can be put on it, and keyword research can be outsourced at a very low price (resources given with the lesson).

### Content-Planung: System für regelmäßiges Publishing (Course B) [L0384, 2021-22]

- [02:34] Arithmetic the teacher states: 2 high-quality pieces per month = 24 high-quality pieces per year ("that starts to sound like a more substantial amount").
- [11:41] Locking the plan: when you lock ideas and the promotion plan into the planner you are locking yourself into doing exactly those things; do not change the plan each time you produce a piece "unless you have a really really good reason"; the point of planning is not to think about it again ("these are the three or four things I'm going to do... I'm not going to deviate from my plan").

### Content-Promotion: Die 12 Methoden zur Reichweitensteigerung (Course B) [L0385, 2021-22]

- [05:15] Early-engagement boost with paid ads: "spend $10 or $20 promoting it" on Google Display / Search / Facebook / Instagram to your target audience to get the ball rolling on content engagement.
- [17:58] For a content search text ad, target the year-labelled term because "search terms are usually a year behind the year you're actually in" (example: "ultimate guide to charcoal grills for 2020" with headline "Don't buy a charcoal grill until you've read this ultimate guide"). Possibly outdated (2019/2020 year examples, only the principle holds).
- [09:28-10:22] Using other people's graphs/research/quotes in a piece is fine as long as you give attribution on the page AND a backlink to the original source.
- [10:22-10:56 + screen 10:56] Outreach rule: "Anytime you link out to a source in your content you should email them and let them know" (template email supplied); many of them will share it to their own network.

### Content-Recherche: Die Vier Fragen (Course B) [L0386, 2021-22]

- [13:31-14:04] The Course B Content Planner can be kept in Excel/Numbers-type spreadsheets, Google Sheets (online version), Trello, or any other online project-management tool; format is free, the recorded fields are what matters.

### Der perfekte Blog-Post (Course B) [L0462, 2021-22]

- [20:40] Internal-link example for ranking: a content page that has earned "100 backlinks" ("which is a lot, you don't generally need that many") links to a collection page; part of the value of those 100 links passes through the internal link, so the collection page ranks better even if it has no links of its own (e-commerce example).

### Duplicate Content vermeiden für besseres SEO-Ranking (Course B) [L0556, 2021-22]

- [11:51] Boilerplate content is present "on about 50% of the sites that I look at" (teacher's frequency estimate).

### E-Commerce Skyscraper: Link Building für Online-Shops (Course B) [L0565, 2021-22]

- [00:41] Google confirmed top three ranking factors: produce content, build links, optimize for RankBrain.
- [06:55] Cost per link is the lowest tier of any link-building activity; [08:51] no high skill needed, ordinary VA can do it; [09:35] 100% white hat, no paid links, no penalty risk.
- [10:22] Outreach builds relationships: co-promotions, guest posts, features.
