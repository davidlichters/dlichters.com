## 03. Suppliers and Fulfillment (page 2 of 2)

### August 2022 Coaching Call: Q&A zu Versand, Ads und Shop-Optimierung (Course A) [L0272, 2022]

- [24:15] About Us page for a Canadian owner with a US mailing address: you do not have to state "US-based"; showing the business mailing address on About Us and Contact is enough.
- [25:52-26:41] Outside the US, search Google for "freight broker" plus your country; FreightCenter is one of dozens of US freight brokers.
- [38:15-39:13] Backlinks: never buy packages of "50 or 100 white-hat links" (Course A stopped about 10 years ago; risk of sandboxing). Worth it as an advanced SEO strategy: services that research one-off link opportunities with tools such as Ahrefs from relevant, high-domain-authority sites.
- [46:49] Supplier spec-sheet PDFs that show the supplier's contact info: keep them for customer service and send after the sale; for the top-20% product pages you optimize further, extract information from the PDFs into the page to make it more robust and unique.
- [59:21-1:00:22] Chandelier example from the blueprint: thousands of products from about 800 suppliers is still niche enough. Niche specificity is judged by whether the same consumer shops for it (chandeliers, stand-up paddle boards, stand-up desks), not by cutting the number of suppliers.
- [1:04:53-1:06:37] Case (Belgium, first fiscal year): 15 silver suppliers, 274 products, 154 orders, 14% margin, blended ROAS 14x, about 90K EUR sales, 0.55% blended conversion rate. Advice: judge conversion only on Google Shopping, Facebook remarketing and Google remarketing (target 2.5% or better); set the date filter to the past year, find the products with a conversion rate above 2.5% in Google Shopping and push more traffic to them.
- [1:09:10] Many brands in a niche being based in California is not a concern: importers bring goods from China into Long Beach and warehouse there because it is most cost-effective.
- [1:09:10-1:10:01] Existing Squarespace store: do not use Squarespace. Start the Shopify 14-day trial, keep the Squarespace store open while building, reuse the assets (logo, homepage images), then delete the Squarespace store and point the domain to Shopify.
- [1:10:53] 360-degree rotating images: possible natively or with apps, but Course A does not use them (about 95%+ of its products lack such assets) and they would likely slow page load.
- [1:16:41-1:17:30] If a supplier confirms it lets some retailers break MAP: do not stop selling it, but shift budget to the most comparable (not knock-off) products from other or new suppliers and promote those more heavily.
- [1:23:26] Selling to the US government with a GSA contract: do not use a supplier's GSA contract number; ask GSA support directly what to do (most likely you apply for your own).
- [1:41:05] Becoming a dropshipper for a Fortune 500 company: same as any brand: confirm it is dropship friendly (works with online-only stores), then call and ask for the person in charge of opening new online accounts.
- [1:43:34-1:44:21] Niche level example: "nightstands" (or stand-up desks, modern office chairs), not "furniture" and not a single product.
- [1:52:42] Dropship automation software (middleman platforms): Course A tested some and says they are not worth it even if free; they limit how big the business can grow.
- [2:08:05] Sitelink extensions: Course A uses them on all its search ads; a store not yet running search ads does not need to worry about "sitelinks active = 0". (context: search text ads, 2022.)
- [2:15:00-2:15:50] Supplier count check: price point $2,500 is fine; about 10 suppliers means niche up a level (fire pits plus fire tables), while about 17 would be fine; ideally above 20. Do not start over.
- [1:58:51-2:00:29] Outranking share dropped: first open the shopping campaign's Change History and revert recent changes that could explain it; then check whether the full daily budget still spends; if not, competitors probably raised bids, so raise your bids slowly every few days until the budget spends again.

### August 2023 Coaching Q&A, Google Shopping, Nische und Supplier-Strategie (Course A) [L0274, 2023]

- [14:25-15:17] Course A cross-checks freight prices every few weeks by entering random shipments on several freight brokers' websites, because prices keep changing.
- [15:17-16:08] Shopify's UPS integration lets you buy labels in Shopify at up to an 80% discount; if connecting fails and Shopify and UPS point at each other, search the exact problem on Shopify's community forum or Reddit.
- [24:12] Dropship agreements (retailer agreements) are always sent by the supplier, never created by the retailer; usually one to two pages covering price policy, how to submit orders, where you may list products and shipping.
- [24:12-25:04] Microsoft Ads suspending new stores before upload: set a weekly calendar reminder and keep trying live chat and phone until a human manually reviews it; reinstatement follows if all policies were followed.
- [17:54] Influencer marketing resource: retreat talk by Chad, owner of Diff Eyewear, a brand grown on influencer marketing (members bonus).
- [29:41-31:08] GA4 settings for remarketing audiences: Admin (gear) > Data settings > Data collection: enable granular location and device data collection, Google signals data collection, allow ads personalization for all regions, and acknowledge the user data collection acknowledgement.
- [32:05] Spend split beta 7% vs alpha 93%: a wide range is normal; if beta traffic is profitable but too small, raise beta bids by about 5 cents.
- [34:38-35:35] Example: average alpha CPC $1.30, average beta CPC $0.40. Keep raising beta CPC only while it brings profitable clicks and sales; stop when spend rises without return; beta should only catch converting long-tail terms, not go broad.
- [38:48-40:37] Exit-intent overlay: email every opt-in manually as the owner ("this is Nathan from Nathanstore.com ... I'd be happy to assist you with placing an order"). To be notified of opt-ins use Zapier, or a Klaviyo automation that emails you "new lead opted in".
- [50:35] Wheelchairs flagged as "health equipment" for Facebook retargeting: remove terminology on the product pages that could trigger that category (check Meta's health-equipment policy) and resubmit those products to the feed.
- [52:25] GoHighLevel: not recommended (Course A has heard it is pushed by many affiliates, MLM-like, and hard to get working).
- [1:18:42-1:19:33] Testing that every opt-in syncs to Klaviyo: use Gmail plus-addressing (name+exitintent@gmail.com) per form; it arrives in your inbox but shows as a separate profile in the CRM.
- [1:27:27] When re-contacting suppliers after launch, stay in the existing product category until you have every quality supplier and product in your niche before reaching into adjacent categories.
- [1:42:44] If competitors sell a product $300 to $400 cheaper (near zero margin), do not pay for Shopping ads on it; keep it on the store for free traffic and include it only in Google and Meta remarketing; focus primary Shopping ads on MAP-enforcing suppliers.
- [1:48:47-1:51:30] Content idea method: search Google for a collection keyword (example "L shape standing desks"); if results show collections and top-10 lists (Wayfair collection with no content, a "10 best" article), add your own better content (e.g. "our top 10 L-shape standing desks") below the products on your collection page, built from what the ranking lists cover.
- [1:51:30-1:53:13] With a large distributor whose competitors sell near zero margin, pricing about 20% above competitors is possible with strong product pages and niche focus; also contact the brands it carries to become an authorized retailer directly for better margins.
- [1:56:36] Add negative keywords weekly (every week is fine).
- [1:57:26-1:58:21] Shopping ranking and price: Course A assumes price does not rank you directly, but a relatively higher price gets fewer clicks, which signals Google that people prefer other listings.
- [2:01:54] Once Microsoft Ads settings are tuned separately (e.g. with a consultant), turn off the auto-sync/import from Google Ads so it does not overwrite them; the setting can be edited without restarting.
- [2:04:40-2:05:31] A wrong "prohibited pharmaceuticals and supplements" flag in the feed app: contact Simprosys support from the app with a screenshot of Merchant Center, and request a review in GMC.
- [2:05:31] Conversion optimization: each module-five element is a fractional gain; combined they give the highest conversion rate, so implement all of them.
- [2:08:09] Facebook Audience Insights: still useful for avatar research but much weaker since the iOS update removed data.

### August 2024 Coaching Call: Lieferanten, Preise und Google Ads (Course A) [L0276, 2024]

- [19:54-21:36] Products suspended in Google Shopping as drugs/dangerous OTC products (example: magnesium cream): search Google Shopping to see whether competitors list it; if they do, contact Google, which (about a decade ago, for home medical devices) sent Course A extra terms of service to sign for restricted categories; otherwise exclude the product from the feed and keep it on the store for organic traffic.
- [28:34-31:31] Six sales in the first three weeks, then none for five weeks: set the Google Ads date range to the first three weeks, check in alpha and beta where sales, spend, impressions, clicks, CTR and keywords came from, then compare with the following five weeks; lower CPC on non-converting products and add negatives. Also search each sold SKU on Google and compare your page split-screen with the top two or three competitors.
- [38:25-39:38] Finding UK/EU suppliers: when searching the niche on Google, open .co.uk sites to find brands on UK stores; lesson 2.7 got an updated SimilarWeb video.
- [42:19] SOPs: keep them in a Google Drive folder, share it view-only with team emails at your store domain (e.g. johnny@yourstore.com); updates reach everyone automatically; do not merge them into one PDF.
- [51:18] Enhanced CPC only appears on campaigns created before Google removed it; new campaigns do not need it; adjust bids at product level instead.
- [52:12-53:05] Becoming a YouTube/TikTok influencer in your niche is not something Course A does (different skill set, lower conversion than targeted ads); get the same effect by paying existing influencers a small percentage as affiliates (lesson "Transferred influence and the Dream 100").
- [1:18:49] Missing-parts example: on a $1,000 item missing a bag of bolts, ship the part fast; if the customer is still upset, offer about $50 back to the original payment method as goodwill.
