### Die Vorteile von Private Labeling (Course B) [L0511, 2021-22]

- [07:26]-[08:42] Owning the supply chain removes out-of-stock surprises: employee reports seven sales in a week, supplier says out of stock, SEO traffic or sales are refunded when everyone else marked it out of stock; "you started this journey to own your life".
- [08:42] Defensibility: nobody can copy your brand, so the fear of being copied or of a brand dropping you is solved by becoming the brand everyone wants.
- [05:12] Add-on economics: a $30 accessory at 30% margin leaves $9 and shipping costs $9, so many sellers do not list accessories; advice: list them anyway, make them profitable, or make/white-label them yourself (pellet grill accessories: pellets, utensils, cedar planks, thermometers, smoking racks, covers, brisket knives, rubs, claws, cast iron) found easily on Alibaba/AliExpress.
- [09:15] Broker multiple for a run-of-the-mill dropshipping business with one traffic source is 2.5x to 3.5x, raised by several traffic sources, email, SEO, a sustainable Snapchat/TikTok flywheel.
- [00:29] Unicorn niches have 50-60% margins; typical dropshipper ends "below 10" net after shipping, card fees and capex.
- [02:19] "Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity".

### Dropshipping-Ansatz: Produkte recherchieren, testen und skalieren (Course C) [L0549, undated]

- [02:06-02:42] Flippa example numbers: store listed at $75,000 claiming about $7,100 profit/month; site age 4 years; $2.7M revenue over 27 months. Another verified listing showed about $1,200 profit/month.
- [03:23] Market order used by the teacher: first Australia and New Zealand instead of US/Canada; if that fails, Germany/Switzerland/Austria (own language).
- [08:14] Bundle offer placement: the Bundle Bear offer sat above the Buy Now button; plan was to retitle it "buy two get 20% off and get a free gift" to raise AOV; the offer right below the fold drove most sales.
