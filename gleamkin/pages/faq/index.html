Questions and answers

How do I use the spray?

Gently lift your dog's upper lip and spray onto the teeth and gums. Follow the directions on the bottle for how much to use and how often.

Do I need a toothbrush?

No. The spray goes straight onto teeth and gums, no brushing needed. You can use it alongside your dog's regular dental care.

My dog doesn't like having its mouth touched. Will this work?

Choose a calm moment and approach your dog gently. The spray suits dogs that accept a brief lift of the lip. Never force it.

What's in the formula?

Water, glycerin, citric acid, 1,2-hexanediol, panthenol, menthol and Lactobacillus ferment lysate. The label on the bottle has the full product information.
