Shipping

Shipping cost

Shipping is free on orders over $49.99. For smaller orders, the shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay.

Order confirmation

Right after you order you get an email with your order number and a summary. If it does not arrive within an hour, check your spam folder or write to hello@gleamkin.com.

Processing time

We pack and hand over your order within 1 to 3 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays start processing on the next business day.

Tracking

As soon as your order ships, you get a second email with a tracking link. Tracking can take 24 to 72 hours to show the first movement.

Delivery time

Most orders arrive 8 to 15 business days after shipping. Packing takes 1 to 3 business days before that. Order today, arrives Nov 25 to Dec 9. Order by for Christmas delivery.

Address

Please check your shipping address before you pay. If you spot a mistake, write to hello@gleamkin.com right away and we fix it if the order has not shipped yet.

Delays

Delivery times are estimates. Carriers, customs, holidays or weather can add a few days. If your parcel is lost, we send a new one or refund you.

Contact

Questions about your delivery: hello@gleamkin.com, answered within 1 business day.
