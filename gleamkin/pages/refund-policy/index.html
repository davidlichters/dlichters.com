Returns and refunds

30-day returns

Changed your mind? Return unopened bottles within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the product price. Arrived damaged or wrong? Send us a photo and we make it right.

How to start a return

Email hello@gleamkin.com with your order number before you send anything back. We reply with the return address and the next steps.

What can be returned

Unopened, unused bottles in the condition you received them, within 30 days of delivery. For hygiene reasons, opened bottles cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or wrong.

Damaged or wrong item

Write to us with your order number and a photo. We send a replacement or refund you.

Return shipping

For a change of mind, return shipping is paid by you and the original shipping cost is not refunded. If we made a mistake, you pay nothing.

When the money arrives

Once the return arrives and is checked, we refund your original payment method. Your bank or card usually shows it a few business days later.

Cancellations

Write to hello@gleamkin.com right away. We cancel for a full refund if the order has not been processed yet.
