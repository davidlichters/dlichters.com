About us

We sell one thing: a talking book that helps children learn to read.

Most children want someone to read with them, and most parents cannot do it every time they ask. This book reads every word your child taps, as often as they want, and waits while they say it back.

Why one product

We would rather do one thing well than fill a shop with toys nobody needs. The Leveled Reading Sound Book needs no app, no Wi-Fi and no batteries. You charge it and read again.
