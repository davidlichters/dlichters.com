Questions and answers

What age is it for?

Ages 3 and up, as printed on the book. It starts with letters and first words and moves up to short stories, so it grows with your child. It contains small parts and is not for children under 3.

How does it work?

Slide the switch on, open a page and press GO. Then tap any word, picture or sentence to hear it read out. On the next page, press GO again.

Does it need batteries?

No. It charges with the USB-C cable that comes in the box.

What's inside?

55 pages: 26 letters, 40 sight words and 40 three-letter words, 200+ everyday words and phrases, 17 leveled stories, 27 children's songs and 4 action songs.

When will my order arrive?

Order today, arrives in 8 to 17 days. Every order ships with tracking.

What if it isn't right for my child?

If the book isn't right for your child, send it back within 30 days of delivery and we refund the full price.

Is it a good gift?

Yes. It comes in its own box. Get two: one for home and one for grandma's house, or one for each child. Order by for Christmas delivery.

Which cover will I get?

The book recently got a new cover. Some books still ship with the cover shown in our photos. The 55 pages inside are the same.

Do you ship outside the US?

Right now we ship to US addresses only.
