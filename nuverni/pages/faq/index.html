Questions and answers

Will it arrive before Christmas?

Order today, arrives in 12 to 24 days. We pack your order within 2 to 5 business days, then it arrives within 7 to 12 business days. Order by for Christmas delivery. Every order ships with tracking.

Is it quiet?

Yes. It is a manual file with no motor, so there is no buzz and no vibration on the paw. You hear only the light scratch of the sheet on the nail.

Can it hurt the quick?

It has no blade, so it never clips. It files the tip a little at a time. Keep to the tip, avoid the quick and the skin around it, and stop if your pet is uncomfortable.

How do I change the sheet?

Peel off the worn sheet and press a fresh one onto the round base. Three replacement sheets come in the box.

How do the 3-pack and 5-pack work?

Choose 3 or 5 above and the discount is applied automatically in your cart, no code needed: 3 nail files cost $99.98 ($33.33 each), 5 cost $149.97 ($29.99 each).

Will it work for my pet?

It suits dogs and cats that let you hold a paw for a few seconds. Filing by hand is slower than clipping, so it is made for touch-ups on the nail tips, not for cutting long nails. Start with short sessions: let them sniff it, file a little, pause and reward.

What is in the box?

1 pet nail file and 3 replacement sheets.

Where do you ship?

To the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, always with tracking. Orders of 2 or more nail files ship free.

What if it isn't right for me?

If the file is not right for you or your pet, send it back within 30 days of delivery and we refund the full price. Arrived damaged or not working? Send us a photo within the same 30 days and we send a replacement or refund you, your choice.

Is it a good gift?
