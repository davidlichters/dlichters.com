Returns and refunds

Our 30-day money-back guarantee

Every nuverni order comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If the file is not right for you or your pet, send it back within 30 days of delivery and we refund the full price. If it arrives damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, we replace it or refund you, at no cost to you.

What we need back

Trying the file at home is fine, that is what the 30 days are for. Please send it back complete with its unused replacement sheets, cleaned of nail dust and not damaged after delivery. Contact us before you send anything back, so we can give you the return address and match the parcel to your order.

How to start a return

  1. Email support@nuverni.com with your order number and a short note on why you are returning it.
  2. We reply within one business day with the return address.
  3. Send the file back with a tracked service and reply with the tracking number.

Return shipping

If you changed your mind, the return shipping is on you; please use a tracked service. If the file arrived damaged, faulty or wrong, we cover the cost, and if we do not need it back, you keep it.

Packs

If you return all files from a 3-pack or a 5-pack, we refund the full order. If you return only some of them, we refund the price you paid per file: $33.33 each from a 3-pack and $29.99 each from a 5-pack.

Shipping costs

Orders of 2 or more ship free. For a single file, the $4.99 shipping fee is refunded when the item arrived damaged, faulty or wrong.

Damaged or faulty items

Send us a photo and your order number within 30 days of delivery, the same 30 days as the guarantee. We send a replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer.

Exchanges

There is one model, so an exchange means a free replacement for a damaged, faulty or wrong item, and we pay the shipping both ways. If you would like more files, simply place a new order.

Cancellations

Changed your mind before your order ships? Email us as soon as possible and we cancel it with a full refund.

Refunds

As soon as your file is back with us, we send the refund to your original payment method. Your bank or card usually shows it a few business days later.

Questions

Write to support@nuverni.com. A real person reads every email and replies within one business day.
