About nuverni

nuverni makes nail care calmer for dogs, cats and the people who love them. Our first tool is a manual pet nail file with a rounded wooden grip and a replaceable abrasive sheet. No motor, no blade and nothing to charge, so you can smooth rough nail tips in a few quiet minutes, at your pet's pace.

Why a manual file

Many pets pull away from clippers, and the buzz of an electric grinder sends others under the bed. A manual file is quiet and has no blade: you hold the paw, file the tip with light strokes and stop whenever your pet has had enough. It is made for small touch-ups between groomer visits, not for cutting long nails, and we would rather say that up front.

What we tell you straight

Filing by hand is slower than clipping. Short sessions work best: let your pet sniff the file, file a little, pause and reward. For very long nails, or if you are unsure, ask your groomer or vet. When a sheet wears, peel it off and press on a fresh one; three replacement sheets come in the box.
