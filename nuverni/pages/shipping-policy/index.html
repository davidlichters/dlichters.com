Shipping

Where we ship

We ship to the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. Orders of 2 or more files ship free. A single file ships for $4.99. Prices are shown in your local currency where available. If your country charges import duties or taxes on delivery, they are paid by the customer, unless your checkout shows them as included.

Order confirmation

Right after you place your order, we email you a confirmation with your order number and a summary of your purchase. If it has not arrived within an hour, please check your spam folder or write to support@nuverni.com.

Processing time

We pack your order and hand it to the carrier within 2 to 5 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays). Please check your shipping address at checkout. If it needs a change, email us before your order ships and we update it.

Tracking

As soon as your order ships, you receive a second email with your tracking number and a link to follow your parcel. Every order ships with tracking.

Delivery time

After your order ships, delivery takes 7 to 12 business days. Counted from the day you order, that is 9 to 17 business days in total. Order today, arrives in 12 to 24 days. Order by for Christmas delivery.

Carriers

We hand every order to a tracked carrier that serves your country. Your shipping email names the carrier and links straight to its tracking page.

Delays

Delivery times are estimates. Customs checks, severe weather and carrier backlogs in the weeks before Christmas can add a few days. If a delay is on our side, we email you. If your parcel has not arrived within the delivery window, or tracking has stopped moving, write to support@nuverni.com with your order number. We check with the carrier, and if your parcel is lost, we send a new one or refund your order.

Out of stock

We do not take custom orders. If an order cannot ship within 2 to 5 business days, we email you the new ship date and you can choose to cancel for a full refund.

Questions

Questions about your delivery? Email support@nuverni.com with your order number. A real person replies within one business day.
